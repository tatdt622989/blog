An **anime pilgrimage in Japan** uses a favorite series as the starting point for visiting its real-world settings or related regions. It can also introduce you to local shopping streets, scenery, and food beyond the usual Tokyo and Kyoto itinerary.

The Anime Tourism Association has published its 2026 selection, adding destinations connected with *Zatsu Tabi: That's Journey*, *Love Live! Superstar!!*, *Maebashi Witches*, *Initial D*, and *The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity*. This guide compares six practical routes by travel time, transport, and the information you should confirm before departure.

<!--more-->

## How to Choose a Destination

Start with a series you care about, then use the [official 2026 selection](https://animetourism88.com/topic/%E3%80%8E%E8%A8%AA%E3%82%8C%E3%81%A6%E3%81%BF%E3%81%9F%E3%81%84%E6%97%A5%E6%9C%AC%E3%81%AE%E3%82%A2%E3%83%8B%E3%83%A1%E8%81%96%E5%9C%B0-88%E3%80%8F2026%E5%B9%B4%E7%89%88-%E9%81%B8%E5%AE%9A%E3%81%AB/) to identify its region. Filter the options using three practical questions:

1. **Is transport convenient?** Walkable locations near frequent trains are best for a first pilgrimage.
2. **Is official information available?** Municipal maps and tourism guides make locations easier to identify and reduce the risk of entering private property.
3. **Is there a good backup plan?** If it rains, a facility closes, or a limited campaign has ended, the town should still offer food, culture, scenery, or hot springs.

The routes below range from a half-day city visit to a multi-day regional trip.

## Route One: Shinagawa, Tokyo, for a First Pilgrimage

One of the new 2026 entries for *The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity* is Maxell Aqua Park Shinagawa in Minato City. It is only a short walk from the Takanawa exit of Shinagawa Station, making it easy to add between a Shinkansen journey, an airport transfer, or a day of conventional Tokyo sightseeing.

Shinagawa works well for a first pilgrimage because the aquarium is about a two-minute walk from the station. Check the [official access information](https://www.aqua-park.jp/en/aqua/access/) and confirm the day's hours and tickets on the main site. Allow one or two hours inside. If you also visit Shibuya that day, choose only one or two nearby stops rather than crossing Tokyo to collect locations.

**Best for:** first-time visitors, families, and travelers with a spare half day.

## Route Two: A Full Day of *Love Live! Superstar!!* in Ushiku

Ushiku entered the 2026 list through *Love Live! Superstar!!*. The city's [official location guide](https://www.city.ushiku.lg.jp/city-promotion/ushiku-kohotaishi-room/page013849.html) compares anime frames with photographs of Ushiku Station, Ushiku Daibutsu, Ushiku Chateau, and Kashiwada Shrine, so newcomers do not need to rely on unverified fan maps.

A practical sequence is **Ushiku Station → Ushiku Chateau → Ushiku Daibutsu**. The station and chateau can be combined on foot. The giant Buddha requires a separate bus journey, and schedules should be checked on the day of travel. If return services are limited, set aside money for a taxi.

Kashiwada Shrine is an active place of worship. Do not block the approach or interfere with people who have come to pray. Limited merchandise, displays, and stamp campaigns may also expire, so confirm current details with the city or organizer before relying on an old travel report.

## Route Three: Two Different Sides of Gunma

Gunma offers two very different pilgrimage styles. Spend the first day walking from JR Maebashi Station with the [official *Maebashi Witches* visitor information](https://www.maebashi-cvb.com/feature/121/top). The station, central shopping streets, Luna Park, and participating businesses can be explored on foot and by local transport. You can also stop at a participating shop for a meal or souvenir.

Plan *Initial D* as a separate second day. Gunma's [official tourism feature](https://gunma-kanko.jp/features/380) covers Mount Haruna, Mount Akagi, and related locations, but the mountain roads are not within walking distance of central Maebashi. A rental car is convenient for Ikaho, Lake Haruna, and stops along the mountain roads. Travelers without mountain or winter-driving experience should use buses and choose a smaller area.

Mountain roads are shared with residents, cyclists, and other visitors. Follow traffic rules, use legal parking, and stay off the roads during fog, ice, fatigue, or severe weather.

## Route Four: Choose Only One Tohoku Area from *Zatsu Tabi*

*Zatsu Tabi: That's Journey* includes settings in Aomori, Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima, and several other prefectures. The anime's [official location map](https://zatsutabi.com/special-map.html) includes Aizuwakamatsu, Matsushima, Hakkoda, and Shin-Hanamaki, but these places are too far apart for a single-day itinerary.

A realistic breakdown looks like this:

- **Fukushima, one or two days:** base yourself in Aizuwakamatsu and combine the castle town with an onsen.
- **Miyagi, one day:** travel from Sendai to Matsushima, then walk the coast and Oshima island.
- **Iwate, one or two days:** connect Shin-Hanamaki, the Miyazawa Kenji Museum, and the Hanamaki onsen area.
- **Aomori, at least two days:** Hakkoda is highly dependent on weather, season, and limited bus schedules.

Related campaigns may have limited dates, so check the official site before departure. Confirm the final train and your accommodation first, then leave extra time for weather or route changes.

## Route Five: *Haikyu!!* Monuments Across Sendai

Sendai has mature official information and a useful subway network. As of August 2026, the [official Sendai Tourism Ambassador site](https://haikyu-sendai.com/) lists four groups of character monuments:

- Shoyo Hinata and Tobio Kageyama at Kamei Arena Sendai, near Tomizawa Station.
- Kei Tsukishima and Tadashi Yamaguchi at the Sendai City Museum.
- Toru Oikawa and Hajime Iwaizumi at Motoyama Engineering Works Aoba Arena.
- Takanobu Aone and Kenji Futakuchi at Akiu Sato Center, added in 2026.

The first three can form a city-based day. Akiu deserves another half day or full day, ideally combined with the hot-spring area. Choosing two or three monuments based on your hotel and transport will leave time for Sendai Castle, local food, or an onsen.

## Route Six: The Ten *ONE PIECE* Statues of Kumamoto

The ONE PIECE Kumamoto Reconstruction Project began as part of recovery efforts after the 2016 Kumamoto earthquakes. The Luffy statue stands outside the prefectural office, while the other nine crew members are placed in nine municipalities. The [official project site](https://op-kumamoto.com/) provides locations and current announcements.

The statues are spread across Kumamoto City, Aso, Ozu, Nishihara, Mashiki, Minamiaso, Takamori, Mifune, and Uto. Attempting to collect all ten in one day by public transport means spending most of the trip waiting for connections. A more enjoyable plan is:

1. Begin in Kumamoto City with the Luffy statue outside the prefectural office and nearby city attractions.
2. Rent a car on day two for Aso and Minamiaso, selecting three to five statues and stopping at local restaurants.
3. Use day three for Uto or Mifune depending on your onward route.

Before renting, confirm license translation requirements, insurance, volcano notices, and road conditions. If you are uncomfortable driving on the left or on mountain roads, use a current licensed tour rather than forcing an overly ambitious self-drive plan.

## A Reliable Planning Method

### Start with Official Maps

Social posts are useful for inspiration, but they may show removed displays, construction zones, or private homes. Build your list from the anime's website, municipal pages, and local tourism associations. Save each location as **essential**, **nearby**, or **rainy-day backup**.

### Plan by Time, Not Distance

Two rural locations may be only ten kilometers apart while their bus runs once every two hours. Include waiting, transfers, meals, and photography in your estimate, and identify the final connection back before adding optional stops. On an inter-prefectural travel day, choose no more than one main pilgrimage area.

### Save Key Information Offline

Keep tickets, hotel addresses, offline maps, and official location guides on your phone. IC cards such as Suica and PASMO are convenient in major cities, but smaller buses, ferries, and shops may still require cash.

## Photography and Community Etiquette

Pilgrimage locations may include stations, residential streets, shrines, and ordinary businesses. Follow posted rules and keep paths clear for residents and other visitors.

- **Never enter private property or school grounds.** Appearing in a work does not grant public access.
- **Stay out of roads, crossings, and platform edges.** No composition is worth endangering yourself or another traveler.
- **Ask before photographing people, shops, or religious interiors.** Put the camera away when photography is prohibited.
- **Do not occupy a location with large equipment.** Take your photo efficiently and keep resident and business access clear.
- **Keep public transport quiet.** Use headphones and do not loudly reenact dialogue inside a train.
- **Carry your rubbish and support local businesses.** Meals, accommodation, and local products all support the host community.

## Final 2026 Pre-Trip Checklist

One week before departure, verify the following:

- Official location pages and facility opening days are still current.
- Limited campaigns have not ended and do not require advance booking.
- Rural buses, ropeways, ferries, and the final return service are operating.
- Weather, road closures, earthquakes, and volcano alerts do not affect the route.
- Your hotel is genuinely close to the first stop on the following morning.
- You have a battery pack, offline map, spare camera battery, and some cash.
- Every day includes time to experience the town without looking through a camera.

## Start with One Series and One Region

For a first trip, choose one favorite series and a region that suits your transport experience. Leave time each day for meals, local shops, and sightseeing instead of rushing between screenshots. Confirm official transport information, opening hours, and campaign dates before departure, and the route will be much easier to manage.
