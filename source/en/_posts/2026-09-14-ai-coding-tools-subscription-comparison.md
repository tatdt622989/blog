## Three points that matter

**Command Code GOAT is the least expensive top-10 entry.** It reaches the list at $10 per month. Its 7.6× figure applies to Qwen3.8 27B; other models have different multipliers.

**There are three clear choices around $20.** Z.ai Coding Lite is $18 at 8.6×, Claude Pro is $20 at 57.5×, and ChatGPT Plus is $20 at 22.4×. Claude and ChatGPT use multi-source usage estimates, while Z.ai is calculated from published allowances and off-peak charging. These are not identical test conditions.

**Three Z.ai plans reach the top 10.** The off-peak multipliers for Lite, Pro and Max are 8.6×, 11.6× and 12.9×. Off-peak means outside Monday–Friday, 14:00–18:00 UTC+8. GLM-5.3 users who can avoid peak hours are more likely to approach the listed figures. [Z.ai plan details](https://docs.z.ai/devpack/overview)

## Claude's September 14 quota change

Starting September 14, Claude Code's standard weekly allowance changes from the promotion's 150% of the original baseline to 125%. That is a reduction of about **16.7%** from the promotion. The table's 57.6×, 57.5× and 56.5× Claude figures are therefore pre-change research estimates, not guarantees after the change. [Official announcement](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2093742321473065266), [official clarification](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2093742322525810912)

New subscriptions and upgrades to ChatGPT Pro $200 have also been paused since September 10. Existing subscribers are unaffected, and the $100 plan remains available. [OpenAI's official explanation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-what-is-chatgpt-pro)

The multiplier measures same-model API-equivalent value, not model capability. Different model scenarios within one plan cannot be added together. For reset details, see the [Claude and Codex usage guide](/en/2026/06/18/claude-codex-usage-limits-guide/).
