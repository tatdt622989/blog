How to compare 11.11 prices at checkout

Match the model, capacity and warranty before comparing listings. Add the item to each cart, apply the vouchers you can use, then include delivery and any required installation. If you’ve saved a price from before the sale, compare it with today’s delivered price. Record any points you already earned and spend on this order separately.

Here’s a hypothetical comparison for the same pair of earbuds.

Store What you pay today Later reward
A NT$1,180 − NT$200 voucher + NT$60 shipping = NT$1,040 100 points
B NT$990 with free shipping None

Store B costs NT$50 less today. If Store A’s 100 points save you NT$100 on a purchase you already plan to make before they expire, Store A’s effective cost drops to NT$940. Budget zero for rewards you might miss because of a registration cap or prize draw, and for points you’re unlikely to spend.

For card offers, check whether the threshold applies per transaction or across orders, what spending counts, which payment plans qualify and when you need to register. A 10% offer capped at NT$100 saves about 3.3% on NT$3,000 of qualifying spending.

Is it worth adding an item to unlock a voucher?

Say your cart is at NT$880, and your voucher takes NT$100 off when you spend at least NT$1,000. Add an NT$150 item and the new total is NT$930 after the voucher. With shipping unchanged, you’re spending NT$50 more to get the extra item. Decide whether you’d pay that much for it on its own.

If you have seller, platform and shipping vouchers, try two combinations at checkout. For items from different sellers, compare the combined cart with separate orders. Count shipping on each order and check whether splitting drops your card payment below the reward threshold. If you might return an item, check how a partial refund affects the reward.

What to buy on Singles’ Day

  • Phones, laptops and appliances. Check the exact model, seller and warranty. For a washing machine or air conditioner, ask about installation, delivery upstairs and removal of the old unit.
  • Diapers, paper goods and cleaning supplies. Compare the delivered price per diaper, sheet or 100 mL. Buy an amount you can store and use before a diaper size change or the next restock.
  • Skincare and food. Count the full-size products you’ll actually use. Compare the bundle with matching sizes sold separately, then check the expiry date against how quickly you’ll get through it.

What to check before paying and after delivery

Confirm that the discount appears in the order summary and check the delivery estimate and return terms. Set a reminder for any separate registration window and save the confirmation. If a caller claims there’s a problem with your order and asks you to use an ATM, hang up and contact support from the order in the official app. In Taiwan, you can call 165 for fraud advice. Do Taiwan online purchases have a seven-day return period?

When you buy online from a business in Taiwan, you generally have seven days after receiving the goods to cancel by returning them or giving the seller written notice. Perishable and made-to-order goods are among the statutory exceptions when the seller has given the required notice. Taiwan consumer protection guidance · Rules on exceptions to the right to cancel
