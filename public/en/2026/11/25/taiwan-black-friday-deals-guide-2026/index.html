Black Friday 2026 from Taiwan: Best Local & Global Deals Worth Buying

If you just survived Taiwan’s 11.11 shopping festival, it is easy to assume the big year-end bargains are over. In reality, 11.11 belongs to local marketplaces like Shopee, momo, and PChome, whereas Black Friday 2026 (November 27) through Cyber Monday (November 30) is when shoppers in Taiwan unlock the widest cross-border price gaps and the deepest annual cuts on global digital services.

Rather than padding this list with US-only doorbusters you cannot ship across the Pacific, this guide looks at the entire global sale strictly through a Taiwanese buyer’s lens: what actually ships cleanly to Taiwan, what delivers genuine savings after shipping and taxes, which zero-customs software and travel promos are worth locking in right now, and how to navigate Taiwan’s NT$2,000 duty limit, six-shipment cap, and credit-card FX traps.

1. Local Taiwan Retail: Costco’s 14-Day Event and the Apple Reality Check

Buying locally during Black Friday makes the most sense for bulky appliances and big-ticket electronics where Taiwan voltage, installation, and domestic warranty matter more than shaving a few percent overseas.

Costco Taiwan Black Friday 2026: Record 14 Days and Tiered Entry

Costco Taiwan doubled its traditional week-long event to 14 consecutive days (November 23 to December 6, 2026), featuring more than 700 discounted items in-store alongside online specials running November 23 to November 29 (with selected items extending to December 6).

For the first time, Costco Taiwan introduced tiered morning entry by membership level:

  • Executive (Black Diamond) members: Early entry daily starting at 07:30.
  • Gold Star and Business members: Standard entry daily starting at 08:30.
  • Purchase cap: Strictly one unit per item per membership card while stocks last.
  • Daily deal previews: Costco Taiwan posts the next day’s featured markdowns at 16:00 on Facebook and Instagram and updates the official app at 17:00. Early waves have focused heavily on 65-inch and 85-inch TVs, washer-dryer combos, Bosch dishwashers, laptops, and bedding.

Skip Apple Taiwan’s Official Store—Shop Authorized Retailers Instead

Every November, Apple fans in Taiwan wonder whether to wait for an official Apple promotion. Here is the short answer: Apple’s November 27–30 US gift card event (offering up to $300 in Apple Gift Cards on eligible products) does not include Apple Taiwan’s online or retail stores. Importing via a US forwarding service adds shipping and 5% VAT, and US Apple Gift Cards cannot be redeemed in Taiwan.

If you want an iPhone, iPad, or Mac during Black Friday in Taiwan, compare authorized resellers such as STUDIO A, Senao Online, and Tsann Kuen, Costco Taiwan, and local e-commerce platforms (PChome 24h, momo, and Shopee Mall). Combining Shopee Mall percentage vouchers (rolling out from November 25) or PChome/momo point multipliers with bank installment promos consistently beats paying full retail.

2. Cross-Border Physical Shopping: Amazon US, Amazon Japan, iHerb & Luxury

Cross-border retail is where Black Friday truly outshines 11.11 for shoppers in Taiwan. Focus on categories with steep local markups so the savings survive shipping and import tax.

Amazon US: 12-Day Black Friday Week and Free Shipping to Taiwan

Amazon US Black Friday Week runs for 12 days from November 19 through November 30, 2026. Before browsing, set your top-left Deliver to address to Taiwan so listings show direct shipping eligibility. Qualifying items marked for AmazonGlobal Free Shipping (commonly triggered at $49 or $60, depending on the product category) ship free once you select the free standard option at checkout.

Four categories consistently yield the biggest savings for Taiwan buyers:

  • High-capacity NVMe SSDs: 2TB and 4TB drives like the WD Black SN850X, Samsung 990 PRO, and Crucial Pro/T-series routinely end up NT$1,000 to NT$2,500+ cheaper than Taiwan retail even after estimated import deposits—ideal for PC upgrades and PS5 storage expansion.
  • Noise-cancelling headphones and Bluetooth speakers: Bose QuietComfort, Sony WH-1000XM, Sennheiser MOMENTUM, and Marshall speakers carry notable local markups in Taiwan and drop sharply on Amazon US.
  • Outdoor gear and insulated drinkware: Patagonia, Columbia, The North Face, and STANLEY tumblers often land at 40% to 50% below Taipei department store pricing.
  • Specialty coffee and kitchen gear: High-end blenders and select espresso machines see deep cuts, though you should always check voltage ratings first.

Amazon Japan: Low Yen, Tax-Free Checkout, and Point-Up Stacking

Amazon Japan Black Friday runs slightly later, using Japan Standard Time (one hour ahead of Taiwan):

  • Early Access Sale: November 27, 00:00 to November 29, 23:59 JST (starts November 26 at 23:00 Taiwan time).
  • Main Black Friday Sale: November 30, 00:00 to December 7, 23:59 JST.

With JPY/TWD hovering near 0.209, direct-to-Taiwan Amazon Japan orders automatically strip out Japan’s 10% consumption tax at checkout. Remember to click the opt-in button on the Point-Up Campaign page before paying—eligible orders can earn up to 11% back in Amazon Points. For region-restricted Japanese appliances or Amazon hardware that will not ship directly, factor in a Taiwan parcel forwarder before checking out.

On both Amazon US and Amazon Japan, install the Keepa browser extension first. Retailers routinely inflate list prices a week before Black Friday; checking the 90-day price chart takes two seconds and prevents falling for fake markdowns.

iHerb Supplements and Tax-Included Fashion (Shopbop, SSENSE, Farfetch)

  • iHerb Taiwan: A staple for vitamins, omega-3s, and protein in Taiwan. During 2026 Black Friday week, iHerb Taiwan is running 25% sitewide discounts and up to 30% off curated collections (test promo codes such as NOVAPAC26 or NOV26HB in your cart to verify eligibility). Keep an eye on Taiwan FDA quantity caps for personal-use capsule and tablet supplements.
  • Shopbop: Live from November 22 evening through December 1 at 17:00 Taiwan time. On Shopbop, enter HOLIDAY on tagged items for 25% off, with free shipping to Taiwan on orders over $100.
  • SSENSE and Farfetch: SSENSE applies straight markdowns without codes, while Farfetch Taiwan adds extra Black Friday savings on sale styles. Both platforms generally display duties-included (DDP) Taiwan pricing, eliminating surprise courier tax bills on delivery.

5 Essential Black Friday Rules for Shoppers in Taiwan

Getting the best headline discount means little if hidden fees wipe out your savings at checkout or customs. Keep these five Taiwan-specific safeguards on hand:

1. Respect the NT$2,000 Duty Threshold and 6-Parcel Cap

Under Taiwan Customs Administration rules, an overseas parcel is duty-free only when two conditions both hold: the customs value is NT$2,000 or less, and you have used no more than 6 duty-free imports in the current half-year (January–June or July–December).

  • Customs value includes shipping: Customs calculates item price + international shipping + insurance. Exceeding NT$2,000 triggers full taxation on the entire parcel (import duty plus 5% VAT), not just the amount above NT$2,000. Most PC parts and electronics carry a 0% tariff rate, leaving only 5% VAT, which still leaves substantial Black Friday savings.
  • Check your remaining quota before buying: If you ordered cross-border packages during 11.11, open EZ WAY or the Customs e-Clearance App first. Starting on your seventh parcel between July 1 and December 31, even an NT$300 order is taxed. Splitting orders from the same sender arriving on the same day will also be merged by customs.

2. Wireless Gear and the NCC NT$750 Inspection Fee Update

Bluetooth headphones, wireless keyboards, game controllers, and Wi-Fi routers fall under Taiwan’s Class 2 controlled RF devices. Good news for Black Friday shoppers: the controversial NT$750 per-item NCC review fee for personal overseas parcels was invalidated by the Legislative Yuan and is not being collected. Standard personal-use quantity limits (typically up to two units) and normal EZ WAY customs confirmation still apply.

3. Always Pay in Foreign Currency—Refuse DCC

At checkout on Amazon, Shopbop, or hotel sites, you will often see a convenient button offering to charge your card in TWD. Always decline it. That option is Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC), and its hidden FX markup commonly adds 3% to 5% on top of bank rates. Pay in USD or JPY directly using a Taiwan credit card that earns 2.5%–3%+ on overseas spend so you more than offset the standard 1.5% foreign transaction fee.

4. Know the 120V (US) and 100V (Japan) vs. 110V (Taiwan) Line

  • Safe to import: Laptops, phones, consoles, headphones, electric toothbrushes, and USB-C gadgets almost universally support 100–240V dual voltage.
  • Think twice: High-wattage heating or motor appliances such as espresso machines, hair dryers, rice cookers, and steam ovens. Running 100V Japanese heating units on Taiwan’s 110V outlets can shorten component life, and 120V US models can underperform—plus local repair parts are rarely covered under warranty.

5. Set Calendar Reminders on Every Subscription the Minute You Pay

Adobe, Proton, NordVPN, Surfshark, Hostinger, and Coursera Plus all price Black Friday promos as introductory first-term rates that auto-renew at double or triple the price. Immediately drop a reminder on your phone calendar seven days before renewal so next November brings no surprise charges.

Bottom Line: The Smartest Order to Shop Black Friday 2026 from Taiwan

  1. Lock in zero-duty digital essentials first: Grab annual discounts on Adobe, VPNs, AI voice tools, online courses, PS Plus, and Steam picks while they last.
  2. Reserve refundable 2027 hotels early: Take advantage of Booking.com, Hotels.com, and Agoda promos before peak-season inventory climbs.
  3. Run a three-point check before overseas hardware checkout: Verify true lows with Keepa, check your remaining semi-annual quota in EZ WAY, and compare your landed cost (shipping + tax) against Costco Taiwan and local retailers.

Calculate shipping, customs, FX, and renewal pricing upfront, and Black Friday 2026 becomes the highest-value shopping window of the year for buyers in Taiwan.
