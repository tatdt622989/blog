Which AI Coding Tool Should You Choose? September 2026 Subscription Comparison

AI coding subscriptions can have similar prices and very different usage value. This September 2026 ranking shows the top 10 entries by value per dollar so you can compare prices and multipliers before subscribing.

Three points that matter

Command Code GOAT is the least expensive top-10 entry. It reaches the list at $10 per month. Its 7.6× figure applies to Qwen3.8 27B; other models have different multipliers.

There are three clear choices around $20. Z.ai Coding Lite is $18 at 8.6×, Claude Pro is $20 at 57.5×, and ChatGPT Plus is $20 at 22.4×. Claude and ChatGPT use multi-source usage estimates, while Z.ai is calculated from published allowances and off-peak charging. These are not identical test conditions.

Three Z.ai plans reach the top 10. The off-peak multipliers for Lite, Pro and Max are 8.6×, 11.6× and 12.9×. Off-peak means outside Monday–Friday, 14:00–18:00 UTC+8. GLM-5.3 users who can avoid peak hours are more likely to approach the listed figures. Z.ai plan details

Claude’s September 14 quota change

Starting September 14, Claude Code’s standard weekly allowance changes from the promotion’s 150% of the original baseline to 125%. That is a reduction of about 16.7% from the promotion. The table’s 57.6×, 57.5× and 56.5× Claude figures are therefore pre-change research estimates, not guarantees after the change. Official announcement, official clarification

New subscriptions and upgrades to ChatGPT Pro $200 have also been paused since September 10. Existing subscribers are unaffected, and the $100 plan remains available. OpenAI’s official explanation

The multiplier measures same-model API-equivalent value, not model capability. Different model scenarios within one plan cannot be added together. For reset details, see the Claude and Codex usage guide.
