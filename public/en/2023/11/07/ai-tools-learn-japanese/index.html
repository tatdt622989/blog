AI Tools to Learn Japanese in 2026: What Works, Prices, and Prompts

The cheapest way to learn Japanese with AI is the voice mode of a general chatbot: ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude all hold spoken conversations in Japanese, and a free account is enough to start practicing speaking, listening, writing corrections and grammar questions. Add a dedicated app such as Aoi or TalkMe only when you want pronunciation scoring, structured lessons or ready-made scenarios. Below are the tools still worth using as of October 2026, followed by copy-paste prompts for reading, listening, speaking, writing and the JLPT.

AI tools for learning Japanese, compared

Checked on October 19, 2026. App prices are from the US App Store and change with region and promotions.

Tool Type Best for Cost
ChatGPT voice General AI Free conversation, role-play, live corrections Free with usage limits; paid plans get more
Gemini Live General AI Practicing on your phone, Google users Built into the Gemini app
Claude voice mode General AI Grammar explanations, rewrites, reading material All plans (beta); counts toward normal usage
Aoi Speak Japanese-only app 1,000+ themed lessons, pronunciation correction, reply hints Free download; $17.99/month, $79.99/year
TalkMe Multi-language app Custom role-play, phrasing suggestions, pronunciation scoring Free download; $15.99/month, yearly from $39.99 on sale
Duolingo Max Course app Daily habit plus video calls with Lily Varies by region and platform; check in the app
Talkio AI Web and mobile Speaking with a fixed AI tutor persona 7-day trial; price shown at checkout

What was verified for each:

  • ChatGPT: voice conversations are available to all logged-in users in the mobile apps and on desktop web (OpenAI Help).
  • Gemini Live: Japanese has been supported since October 2024, with up to two languages per device (Google Japan blog). It runs in the mobile app only, not the web app (Gemini Help).
  • Claude: voice mode is a beta on every plan, including Free; languages other than English are still in beta. Switch to Japanese under Settings → General → Voice → Language (Claude Help).
  • Duolingo Max: Duolingo’s blog says Japanese Video Call launched on iOS first (Duolingo blog); confirm Android availability in the app before subscribing.
  • Aoi and TalkMe: both were still receiving updates in 2026, and the prices above come from their App Store listings.

Tools from the 2023 version of this article that no longer make the cut: the SpeakAI site is offline, the Voice Control for ChatGPT extension is redundant now that ChatGPT has built-in voice, and the TalkBerry site no longer lists Japanese.

Use one general AI for two weeks first. If you are still speaking every day, then decide whether a paid app is worth it.

Listening: generate a shadowing script

Then switch to voice mode, ask the AI to read it slowly (“ゆっくり読んでください”), and shadow along. AI voices do not always get pitch accent right, so check key words in the University of Tokyo’s OJAD accent dictionary.

JLPT prep: let AI explain, use official questions

The next JLPT is on Sunday, December 6, 2026 (JLPT official site). AI-written questions can have the wrong format or wrong answers, so practice with the official sample questions and published mock tests, and use AI to explain mistakes and track weak points:

In the last month before the test, ask the AI each week to turn your list of wrong answers into review cards for your five most-missed grammar points.

Three things to watch

  1. AI makes mistakes, especially with keigo nuance, dialects and kanji readings. Check anything important in a dictionary or textbook.
  2. Set the output format: state your level, native language and the format you want, or the AI will over-explain or mix languages.
  3. A fixed daily slot beats more tools: 15 minutes of voice practice every day does more than three overlapping subscriptions.
